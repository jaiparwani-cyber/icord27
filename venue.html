Venue & Travel

ICoRD'23 will be held at the National Science Seminar Complex in the campus of Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore, India.

Indian Institute of Science is one of the leading academic institutions in India with a lush green campus north of Bangalore. Bangalore in January will be cool (15-20 deg C) and pleasant. Bangalore offers a convenient base to explore the cultural and scenic attractions, in the southern and western parts of India; the temples at Belur, Halebedu and Hampi being the most noteworthy.

Bengaluru is connected by air, rail, and road to all the major cities of the country. Also, one can fly directly to Bengaluru from several international airports.

IISc is about 35 km from the Bengaluru International Airport named Kempegowda International Airport (BLR). One could hire either an Airport Taxi near the terminal exit or book an Uber/Ola cab. Cabs will typically charge around Rupees 700-1400 (depending on time, availability and type of cab). A bus service operated by Bengaluru Metropolitan Transport Corporation (BMTC) runs from the airport to various areas in Bengaluru city. Service ‘KIA – 10’ will drop you to a bus stop near IISc. The institute is known as ‘Tata Institute’ to the locals and the bus stop to get down at while coming to IISc from the airport is thus called as ‘Tata Institute Bus Stop’. Cost for the bus service is around Rupees 250. Additional information on the airport can be found at its official website Bengaluru Airport and more information regarding BMTC and route timings for KIA-10 can be found on its official website: BMTC

The City Railway Station (SBC) is about 7 km from the IISc campus. One can exit the train station from platform number 1 and board a prepaid taxi/auto rickshaw/Uber/Ola (costs around Rupees 150-200). Majestic bus station (7 km from IISc) is the central bus stand in Bengaluru and is just opposite the city railway station. Bus numbers from Bangalore City Bus Station are 273, 276, 99A, 99B, 272, 252.

The Yeshwantpur railway station is another major railway station in Bengaluru and is located less than 2 km from the IISc campus. One can take a ‘pre-paid’ auto from the counter located outside the exit gate. Booking an Uber/Ola is a more convenient option.
