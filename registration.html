Information for Registration

  1. Each individual who wishes to attend ICoRD’27 needs to register by filling out the registration form (see further below) and by paying the registration fee (for exceptions, see point 4. below). The type of registration fee will be decided by the affiliation and not nationality.
  2. For each paper, at least one author must register by paying the registration fee.
  3. A single registration fee can be used by an author for up to two of his/her papers. If an author has more than two papers, he/she needs to pay separate registration fees for the additional papers.
  4. Invited delegates still need to fill-out the registration form but do not need to pay the registration fee .
  5. Please make a note of transaction ID and payment reference number for future reference.
  6. See FAQ below for more clarification.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

  1. My paper ID is 106. There are three authors for this paper. We all want to participate in the conference. How many registrations will be required?
    Answer: Each person who wishes to participate in the conference will have to register by paying the full conference fee separately. In your case, three separate registrations are required.
  2. I have two different papers that have been accepted for ICoRD’27; in each, I am the single author, and I will present both papers myself. Do I have to register twice?
    Answer: No. One registration fee covers up to two papers. However, each paper must be registered separately in the conference submission system.
  3. I have three different papers that have been accepted for ICoRD’27. Do I have to register thrice?
    Answer: No. One registration fee covers up to two papers. Therefore, for three papers, two registrations are required.
  4. I have four different papers that have been accepted for ICoRD’27. Do I have to register four times?
    Answer: No. One registration fee covers up to two papers. Therefore, for four papers, two registrations are required.
  5. My co-author and I have one paper in ICoRD’27. How many registrations will be required for both of us to attend the conference?
    Answer: A total of two registrations will be required, as each person who wishes to attend the conference must register separately.
  6. My co-author and I have two papers in ICoRD’27. How many registrations will be required for both of us to attend the conference and present the papers?
    Answer: A total of two registrations will be required, one for each person. One registration fee covers up to two papers.
  7. I am an Indian national having affiliation in a foreign institution/organization. Under which category should I pay?
    Answer: If you are affiliated with a foreign institution/organization, you need to register under the International category irrespective of your nationality. Similarly, if you are affiliated with an Indian/SAARC institution or organization, you should pay the registration fee applicable to Indian/SAARC participants, irrespective of your nationality.
  8. One of my co-authors has an international affiliation. I have already paid the registration fee for the submission and my registration. What fee should my co-author pay?
    Answer: Each participant has to pay the registration fee applicable to the type of affiliation (Indian/SAARC or International) and the applicable membership category (DS/DRS, DeScIn Member, or Reviewer). Kindly note that your co-author does not need to pay extra page charges for the paper if you have already paid these charges as part of your registration.
  9. I am an invited delegate to ICoRD’27 (Keynote Speaker, ICONN Awardee, Co-Chair, member of the Steering Committee, etc.). Do I have to pay a registration fee to attend ICoRD’27?
    Answer: If you are a (co-)author of a paper to be presented at ICoRD’27 for which no other (co-)author has registered by paying the registration fee, you must register by paying the applicable registration fee. If that is not the case (you have no paper to be presented at ICoRD’27 or your co-author has already registered for the paper by paying the registration fee), you do not need to pay a registration fee to attend the conference.
  10. Can I transfer my registration to my co-author if I am unable to attend the conference?
    Answer: Yes. You can transfer your registration to your co-author before the start of the conference, provided that the registration categories match, including nationality, type of participant, and other applicable criteria. You need to inform the organisers at least one day before the start of the conference so that the registration can be transferred.
  11. Can I get my registration fee refunded if I am unable to attend the conference?
    Answer: No. Registration fees are not refundable.
